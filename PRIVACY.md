# Privacy

This is a single-user, self-hosted personal finance app. It reads the owner's own bank and payment accounts, with the owner's consent, and stores the data only on the owner's own computer. No data is shared with or stored by any third party, and nothing is used for advertising or analytics. Access can be withdrawn at any time with the account provider, and the stored data deleted by the owner.
